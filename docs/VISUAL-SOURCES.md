## Figma reference

https://www.figma.com/design/lN7WHuD3nhqEHzeWQsDWA2/Student-Hub-Website--Protatype-?node-id=0-1&p=f

The homepage follows the reference's RTL two-column hero, teal gradient headline, white-framed campus photograph, about/vision/mission row, teal/mint service grid, university cards, dark statistics band, benefit cards and lower-page calls to action. Shared spacing, colors and navigation use the same direction across the site.

This is not a claim of pixel-perfect reproduction. The exact Figma photograph and source assets were not available for export in the guest view. The replacement below is illustrative, not a partner university. Reference-only commerce, tickets and housing have not been disguised as functional services. Unsupported user counts, accreditation and partnership claims were intentionally not copied. Numbers are queried from actual published demo content.
