# Roadmap

Owner-approved direction, kept deliberately simple. No dates or extra initiatives have been added.

## Early phases

- Improve user experience and UI.
- Improve the platform's infrastructure.
- Begin real operation.
- Intended initial university scope: Qena and Luxor, academic year 2027/28.

## Future direction

- Expand the platform toward a broader set of SaaS services.
- Develop Gumball, the mascot and future bot name, into an AI virtual assistant inside the site.
- Expand to private schools and take responsibility for their system side.

Detailed sequencing, budgets and delivery dates are not yet decided. The current Gumball name does not imply a working AI integration.
