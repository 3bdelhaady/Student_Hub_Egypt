# Privacy and intended disclosures

## Data categories

Account identity/contact and password hash; session metadata; university/faculty affiliation and status; institution/organization assignments; student codes; profile and learning activity; applications/support/order requests; scoped roster exports; media and audit records. Exact fields are in the database inventory, not a promise that every field is necessary for real operation.

National-ID functionality was removed from the active schema. That does not establish erasure from provider backups or historical copies. Do not restore old national-ID features from obsolete documentation.

## Student codes and CSVs, explicit decision

On 25 November 2026, after the disclosure tradeoff was explained, the owner confirmed keeping current student codes and roster CSVs as they are. Code prefixes expose account type and gender: ST/SA/PS/AD plus M/F/X, followed by random text. Scoped team CSV exports include names and codes. Preserve this as an intended product decision, not an accidental implementation omission. It is not a legal-consent determination or permission to publish rosters openly.

Exports remain limited to authorized management scope and safe download handling. Team/name-code rosters must not gain unrelated fields without an approved requirement. Other export routes have different existing schemas: the ticket-registration export includes contact/affiliation details. Review each export separately rather than assume every CSV is minimal.

## Real-operation decisions still required

Legal operator details, privacy contact, jurisdiction/lawful basis, minors handling if private schools are added, retention/deletion periods, access/export/removal workflow, incident notification, advertising-data use, provider processing and contracts are not yet decided. "Startup" does not establish legal registration or compliance. Annual university subscriptions are a business model, not permission to share student records.

No analytics-based behavior monitoring is claimed. Advertising income refers to Student Hub-managed social channels, not silent in-platform profiling. Future Gumball AI must not receive student data until its scope, provider and privacy policy are decided.
